# Community Avian and Air Quality Network for Observing Pollution Year-round (CANOPY)
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a2232a6-50a2-4f5d-b294-990c27e94cbc)

CANOPY is an innovative environmental sensing initiative that deploys state-of-the-art smart sensors to collect and publicly share real-time environmental data year-round. Designed to support public health, ecological research, and emergency response, the network delivers continuous, high-resolution insights into local atmospheric and ecological conditions.
Key features of the CANOPY project include:
Avian Biodiversity Monitoring: Automated birdsong identification every 3 seconds to track changes in local bird populations.
Comprehensive Meteorological Sensing: Real-time monitoring of barometric pressure, temperature, humidity, dew point, rainfall, wind speed, and wind direction at 1-second intervals to support weather-aware health alerts and emergency planning.
Optical Monitoring: Continuous measurement of ultraviolet (UV), visible, and near-infrared (NIR) light levels to inform outdoor safety decisions and support optical environmental analysis.
Advanced Air Quality Tracking: Second-by-second monitoring of particulate matter (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ to assess pollution exposure and respiratory risk, especially for sensitive groups.
Additional Environmental Indicators: Detection of ground-level ozone, lightning strikes, and ambient radiation to broaden public environmental awareness.
Sky Imaging: Upward-facing smart cameras capture real-time sky conditions for cloud analysis and visual context.
Resilient, Smart Power Management: Intelligent power systems ensure reliable 24/7 operation across all sensor platforms.
Open Data and Visualization: All data is openly accessible through comprehensive public dashboards, empowering community science, education, and policy-making.
Scalable Architecture: The modular system design supports the seamless integration of additional sensors and expansion to new locations.
CANOPY exemplifies a community-first approach to environmental intelligence, combining biodiversity, weather, and pollution data into a unified, scalable, and publicly accessible platform.

STRASS Smart TRaffic Awareness Sensing System
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7cbbf2e-74a1-4f79-88d8-048cc6079bf7)

STRASS is a real-time environmental and traffic monitoring platform deployed at traffic intersections to support urban planning, public health, and dynamic traffic control. Using advanced smart sensors, the system continuously collects and shares high-resolution environmental data in public dashboards.
Key features include:
Air Quality Monitoring: Continuous measurement of particulate matter (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ every second to monitor pollution in high-traffic zones.
Avian Monitoring: Automated birdsong identification every 3 seconds to track biodiversity in urban areas.
Meteorological Sensing: Real-time tracking of barometric pressure, temperature, humidity, and dew point every second for health and emergency applications.
Noise Monitoring: Real-time ambient noise level sensing to assess sound pollution and its impact on health and urban environments.
Traffic System Integration: Designed for seamless integration with traffic lights to enhance situational awareness and enable intelligent traffic flow adjustments.
Use Cases:
Dynamic Tolling: Adjust congestion tolls in real time based on pollution levels to incentivize cleaner traffic patterns and reduce environmental impact.
Adaptive Traffic Control: Modify signal timing based on real-time environmental data to reduce idling, traffic buildup, and localized emissions.
Public Health Alerts: Trigger automated warnings in high-risk pollution zones, particularly for vulnerable populations.
Urban Planning: Support data-driven infrastructure planning by mapping long-term pollution and noise trends.
Environmental Justice: Empower communities with open access to localized air and noise quality data to inform advocacy and policy.


Mints Automobile Sensing Kit (MASK)
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/42134c4a-33e1-42f0-b64f-f0dccf8ee153)
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9cebcfb8-5ed7-4740-bf34-56478a8e9fec)

MASK is a compact, vehicle-mounted environmental sensing system designed to attach directly to a vehicle’s number plate, enabling mobile, real-time environmental data collection across urban and rural areas. It leverages advanced smart sensors to publicly share high-resolution data critical for air quality monitoring, health research, and environmental justice.
Key Features:
Mobile Meteorological Sensing: Continuous monitoring of barometric pressure, temperature, humidity, and dew point every second while the vehicle is in motion.
Air Quality Monitoring: Real-time measurement of particulate matter (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ every second to identify pollution exposure and hotspots.
Location and Motion Tracking: Integrated GPS and IMU provide precise geolocation and movement data to contextualize environmental readings.
Portable Power: Internal battery supports over 6 hours of continuous operation for flexible, autonomous deployment.
License Plate Mounting: Securely mounts to the vehicle’s number plate for unobtrusive installation and easy transfer between vehicles.

Use Cases:
Mobile Pollution Mapping: Monitor environmental conditions dynamically along traffic routes, school zones, and industrial corridors.
Public Health Surveillance: Support real-time exposure studies by linking environmental data with time and location.
Disaster Response: Deploy rapidly in emergency scenarios to assess environmental risks such as wildfires or chemical spills.
Environmental Equity & Policy: Inform data-driven planning and advocacy by revealing pollution disparities across communities.




WESTA (Wearable Environmental Sensing for Tracking Air)
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f45cb310-932e-4e3f-a303-166a887075e0)
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9158e88f-dcc5-4ec7-be42-1f08f5af7efe)

WESTA is a personal, wearable sensor system that helps you understand the air you breathe in real time. Designed for everyday use, WESTA tracks environmental conditions and your location to provide personalized insights into pollution exposure and weather—whether you’re walking, biking, commuting, or exercising outdoors.
What WESTA Measures
Air Quality: Detects harmful pollutants like particulate matter (PM0.1 to PM10) and carbon dioxide (CO₂) every second.
Weather Conditions: Monitors temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and dew point to help you stay weather-aware.
Location & Motion: Built-in GPS and motion sensors track your route and activity to map environmental exposure.
Rechargeable Power: Runs for over 4 hours on a single charge—lightweight and hassle-free.

How It’s Worn

WESTA is designed to be worn either on the arm using an adjustable band (like a phone armband) or on the chest using a Velcro patch on a vest. It’s small, silent, and easy to wear during daily routines or outdoor activities.


What’s Coming Next
A future add-on band will track your body’s response to the environment, including:
Heart rate and heart rate variability
Skin temperature
Blood oxygen levels
Sweat response

Together, these tools will help answer questions like:
How does air quality affect my health in real time?

Why WESTA Matters
Breathe Safer: Get real-time alerts when air quality is poor so you can protect yourself.
Make Smarter Choices: Know when and where it’s safest to exercise or spend time outdoors.
Support Health & Justice: Contribute to public data that highlights environmental inequality in your community.
