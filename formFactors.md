# 🌳 Community Avian and Air Quality Network for Observing Pollution Year-round (CANOPY)

![CANOPY Sensor Node](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a2232a6-50a2-4f5d-b294-990c27e94cbc)

**CANOPY** is an innovative, community-centered environmental sensing initiative that deploys smart, modular sensor systems to collect and share real-time data year-round. Built to support public health, ecological research, and emergency response, CANOPY offers continuous, high-resolution insights into local atmospheric and biodiversity conditions.

## 🔍 Key Features

- **🦜 Avian Biodiversity Monitoring**  
  Automated birdsong identification every 3 seconds tracks local bird populations and migration patterns.

- **🌦️ Meteorological Sensing**  
  Monitors barometric pressure, temperature, humidity, dew point, rainfall, wind speed, and direction at 1-second intervals for weather-aware alerts.

- **💡 Optical Monitoring**  
  Continuously measures UV, visible, and near-infrared (NIR) light levels to support outdoor safety and ecological research.

- **🌫️ Air Quality Monitoring**  
  Tracks particulate matter (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ every second to identify pollution hotspots and assess respiratory risks.

- **🌍 Additional Environmental Indicators**  
  Detects ground-level ozone, lightning strikes, and ambient radiation to expand environmental situational awareness.

- **📷 Sky Imaging**  
  Upward-facing cameras capture real-time cloud conditions for atmospheric context and climate analysis.

- **🔋 Smart Power Management**  
  Intelligent energy systems ensure 24/7 reliable operation across all sensing platforms.

- **📊 Open Data & Visualization**  
  All data is freely available through interactive public dashboards—empowering community science, education, and policy-making.

- **🧩 Scalable & Modular Design**  
  Easily integrates new sensors and expands to additional locations as the network grows.

> **CANOPY** embodies a community-first vision of environmental intelligence—uniting data on biodiversity, air quality, and weather into a single, accessible platform.

# 🚦 STRASS – Smart TRaffic Awareness Sensing System

![STRASS Sensor Setup](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7cbbf2e-74a1-4f79-88d8-048cc6079bf7)

**STRASS** is a real-time environmental and traffic monitoring system deployed at traffic intersections to inform urban planning, public health, and smart traffic control. Through advanced sensing and open data, STRASS enables dynamic, high-resolution insight into pollution and traffic patterns.

## 🔍 Key Features

- **🌫️ Air Quality Monitoring**  
  Second-by-second measurement of PM and CO₂ to assess pollution in high-traffic areas.

- **🦉 Avian Monitoring**  
  Detects birdsong every 3 seconds to track biodiversity in urban spaces.

- **🌡️ Meteorological Sensing**  
  Monitors key atmospheric variables in real time for public health and safety.

- **🔊 Noise Monitoring**  
  Captures ambient sound levels to identify noise pollution and its effects on well-being.

- **🚦 Traffic Light Integration**  
  Syncs with existing systems to enable adaptive, environmentally responsive traffic flow.

## 🚀 Use Cases

- **Dynamic Tolling**  
  Adjust congestion pricing based on pollution levels to encourage cleaner commuting.

- **Adaptive Signal Timing**  
  Optimize light cycles to reduce idling and emissions.

- **Public Health Alerts**  
  Automatically issue warnings during pollution spikes, especially in vulnerable zones.

- **Urban Planning Support**  
  Inform long-term infrastructure decisions using environmental trend data.

- **Environmental Justice**  
  Provide communities with transparent, localized environmental data to support equity-focused policy.

# 🚗 MASK – Mints Automobile Sensing Kit

![MASK Front Mount](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9cebcfb8-5ed7-4740-bf34-56478a8e9fec)

**MASK** is a compact, vehicle-mounted environmental sensing unit designed for mobile deployment. Mounted on a vehicle’s license plate, it collects real-time environmental and GPS data across urban and rural routes.

## 🔍 Key Features

- **🌦️ Mobile Weather Sensing**  
  Continuously tracks pressure, temperature, humidity, and dew point while on the move.

- **🌫️ Mobile Air Quality Monitoring**  
  Measures PM (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ every second to map pollution hotspots.

- **📍 Location & Motion Tracking**  
  GPS and IMU data enhance context and precision in environmental measurements.

- **🔋 Portable Power**  
  Internal battery offers 6+ hours of operation for flexible deployment.

- **🛠️ Easy Installation**  
  License plate mount allows secure, tool-free installation and quick transfers between vehicles.

## 🚀 Use Cases

- **Pollution Mapping on the Move**  
  Track air quality around schools, parks, and industrial zones.

- **Health Research & Surveillance**  
  Enable mobile studies of environmental exposure in real time.

- **Rapid Emergency Response**  
  Deploy to disaster zones for on-the-ground environmental diagnostics.

- **Environmental Justice**  
  Visualize disparities in pollution exposure to guide equitable policymaking.

# 🎽 WESTA – Wearable Environmental Sensing for Tracking Air

![WESTA on Vest](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f45cb310-932e-4e3f-a303-166a887075e0)  
![WESTA on Armband](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9158e88f-dcc5-4ec7-be42-1f08f5af7efe)

**WESTA** is a personal, wearable environmental sensor system that provides real-time insight into the air you breathe. Lightweight and rechargeable, it’s designed for daily wear while walking, biking, or exercising outdoors.

## 🔍 What WESTA Measures

- **🌫️ Air Quality**  
  Detects PM (PM0.1 to PM10) and CO₂ every second.

- **🌡️ Weather**  
  Tracks temperature, humidity, pressure, and dew point.

- **📍 GPS & Motion**  
  Logs location and activity data to map your exposure.

- **🔋 Rechargeable Power**  
  Over 4 hours of use per charge.

## 🧑‍🏃 How to Wear It

- **Armband** – Comfortable for everyday use during exercise.  
- **Vest Patch** – Discreet chest-mounted option with Velcro attachment.

## 🔮 What’s Coming Next

WESTA+ will include biometric sensors for:

- Heart rate and variability  
- Skin temperature  
- Blood oxygen levels  
- Sweat response

## 💡 Why WESTA Matters

- **Stay Safer** – Get alerts when air quality dips below healthy levels.  
- **Make Smarter Choices** – Know when and where it's safe to be outdoors.  
- **Advance Justice** – Contribute to open datasets that expose environmental inequalities.
